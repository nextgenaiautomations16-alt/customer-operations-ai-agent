# Support Channels, Hours and Escalation

## Hours and channels
Support is available Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC by email and in-app chat. There is no phone support.

## First response targets
High priority cases get a first response within 1 business hour. Medium priority cases within 8 business hours. Low priority cases within 2 business days.

## Repeat contacts
A customer who has contacted support twice or more about the same unresolved issue is escalated to a support lead, who takes over the case and replies personally.
