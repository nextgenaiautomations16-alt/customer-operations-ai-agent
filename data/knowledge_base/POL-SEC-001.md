# Suspected Unauthorized Access

## Always escalate
Reports of a hacked or compromised account, unauthorized access, stolen credentials, a data breach alert, or charges the customer does not recognize are escalated to the security team immediately. Support does not investigate these alone.

## What to tell the customer
Advise the customer to change their password, sign out of all sessions and revoke API keys they do not recognize. Do not share account details until identity is verified. The security team responds to confirmed compromises within 1 hour.

## Never promise
Do not confirm whether an account was breached and do not promise a refund of unauthorized charges before the security team has completed its review.
