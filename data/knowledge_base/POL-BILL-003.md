# Refund Eligibility

## Monthly plans
Monthly plan payments are not refundable, except for confirmed duplicate charges and confirmed billing errors made by Acme Cloud.

## Annual plans
Annual plan customers can request a prorated refund within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. After 30 days an annual plan is not refundable.

## Approval
Every refund needs approval from a billing specialist. Support agents cannot issue refunds themselves. The customer is told that the request is being reviewed, not that it is approved.
