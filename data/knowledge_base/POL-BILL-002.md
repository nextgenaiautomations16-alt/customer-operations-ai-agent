# Invoices, Plans and Payment Methods

## Invoices and receipts
Invoices and payment receipts are available under Billing, then History, and can be re-sent to the billing email on request. The account owner can change the billing email and the company name shown on invoices in Billing settings. Support can re-issue an invoice with a corrected company name.

## Invoice disputes and unrecognized charges
A customer may dispute an invoice line within 60 days of the invoice date. Support collects the invoice number and the disputed line and passes it to the billing team for review. Charges for add-ons the customer says they never ordered are reviewed by the billing team before any credit is given.

## Plans and billing cycles
The Starter plan is billed monthly. The Pro plan can be billed monthly or annually, and annual Pro plans are charged upfront for twelve months. Charges are made on the same calendar day of each billing cycle.

## Payment methods
Acme Cloud accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Bank transfer is available for annual Pro plans only. Other payment methods are not supported.
