# Out-of-Scope Requests

## Advice support cannot give
Support does not give legal, tax or financial advice. Customers with questions about the enforceability of a contract should speak to their own advisor.

## Contract changes
Support cannot change contract terms, negotiate custom pricing or make exceptions to the terms of service.

## Feature requests
Feature requests are logged through the product feedback portal. Support does not promise that a feature will be built or give delivery dates.
