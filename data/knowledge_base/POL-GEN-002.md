# Data Export and Account Deletion

## Exporting data
Account owners can export their data under Settings, then Data, then Export, in CSV or JSON format. Large exports are emailed as a download link that stays valid for 7 days.

## Deleting an account and personal data
Account deletion and personal data erasure requests must come from the account owner. They are processed within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Data under a legal hold cannot be deleted until the hold ends.
