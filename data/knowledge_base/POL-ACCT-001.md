# Account Lockouts and Suspensions

## Lockout after failed sign-ins
After 5 failed sign-in attempts an account is locked for 30 minutes and unlocks automatically. Customers can wait, or use the password reset link to sign in sooner.

## Suspension after a failed payment
If a payment fails, the customer has a 7 day grace period. After that the account is suspended. A suspended account is restored once a valid payment method is on file and the outstanding invoice is paid. Restoring access normally happens within one business day of a successful payment.

## Manual unlock by support
When a customer cannot sign in for another reason, support verifies identity by asking the account owner to confirm the registered email address. Unlock requests are carried out by a support lead after identity verification. A support agent cannot unlock an account alone.
