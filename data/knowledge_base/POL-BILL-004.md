# Cancelling a Subscription

## How to cancel
The account owner can cancel at any time under Settings, then Subscription, then Cancel plan. Only the account owner can cancel. Support can explain the steps but cannot cancel on behalf of someone who is not the account owner.

## What happens after cancelling
Access continues until the end of the current billing period. The subscription then ends and no further charges are made. Monthly plans receive no partial refund for unused days.
