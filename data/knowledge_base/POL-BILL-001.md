# Duplicate Charges and Refunds

## Verifying a duplicate charge
When a customer says they were charged twice for the same subscription period, support checks the billing system for two successful payments on the same invoice and period. A pending authorization that disappears within 5 business days is not a duplicate charge and needs no refund.

## Refunding a confirmed duplicate
A confirmed duplicate payment is refunded in full to the original payment method. Refunds of confirmed duplicate charges must be approved by a billing specialist before they are issued. Refunds normally appear on the customer's statement within 5 to 10 business days.

## What to tell the customer
Tell the customer that the charge is under review and give the expected refund timeline. Never promise a refund before a billing specialist has approved it.
